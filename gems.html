Gemstones act as cosmic amplifiers. Wearing them without chart analysis can bring unintended results.

When You SHOULD Wear a Gemstone

Positive Cosmic Alignment
  • Yogakaraka Planet Boost: When a beneficial planet in your horoscope needs extra strength to produce success and wealth.
  • During Favorable Dasha: Wearing the stone of a planet currently ruling your Dasha/Antardasha to maximize its positive results.
  • Proper Muhurat & Vidhi: Wear only after proper Pooja, Abhishekam, and Chanting during the auspicious planetary hora.
  • Right Metal Contact: Ensure the gemstone touches your skin and is set in its appropriate metal (Gold, Silver, or Copper).
✕

When You MUST NOT Wear a Gemstone

Strict Caution Required
  • For Malefic / Trika Houses: NEVER wear gems for planets owning 6th, 8th, or 12th houses without expert consultation.
  • Opposing Planetary Gems: Never pair incompatible gems together (e.g., Neelam/Ruby, Ruby/Rahu-Ketu stones) as they create energy conflicts.
  • Cracked or Glass-Filled Gems: Avoid synthetic, glass-filled, or heat-treated commercial gems; they lack cosmic prana and give negative effects.
  • Without Horoscope Verification: Never wear a gemstone solely based on Sun Sign, Moon Sign, or general internet recommendations.
